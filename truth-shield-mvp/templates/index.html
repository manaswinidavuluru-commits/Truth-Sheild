FORENSIC TRANSPARENCY

How Truth Shield Verifies Authenticity

Truth Shield adheres to the ethical imperative of digital forensics: we never invent evidence or claim certainty without empirical backing.

📰

Accredited Fact-Checking

Cross-references claims against verified International Fact-Checking Network (IFCN) repositories, including Snopes, PolitiFact, and Reuters, alongside open encyclopedia entities.

Authoritative Index
🔬

Error Level Analysis (ELA)

Resaves JPEG media at a standardized 90% quality to compute pixel-by-pixel compression variance. Foreign spliced elements compress differently, revealing manipulation hotspots.

Pillow Engine
⏱️

Temporal Continuity

Demuxes video frames to inspect inter-frame pixel deltas, sudden transition spikes, and focus stability (Laplacian variance) across sampled keyframes.

OpenCV Vision
🛡️

Strict Uncertainty Handling

When media has stripped metadata or when third-party fact-checks are absent, Truth Shield honestly displays "Unable to verify" instead of fabricating a verdict.
